# build-time-bench-of-the-product


Track 6, N4: one committed run of what the product costs to compile over a registry of more than a hundred columns, as an arm of the bench harness, so the number stands in the harness's results layout rather than in an ad-hoc spike. The two spikes that measured compile time before (`202611140945_profile-arms-adhoc`, `202611140950_hlist-scaling-adhoc`) are ad hoc, and both are written against a product shape that `15a9531` replaced.

The vehicle is the one the mockspace sketch `202611160019_a-bench-arm-that-measures-a-build` showed working: the harness is unchanged, and an arm shells out to cargo inside `timed!`. One bench, `product-build`, one arm, `from_clean`, points 32, 64, 128 and 256 columns. A call writes the fixture's generated source for its point and cleans the fixture package alone, both untimed, so `notko-hlist` stays built and the timed block is the fixture's own compile. The fixture models what a consumer's registry asks of the product: a column marker per member, the row product through `Val` with its value, one `ContainsAll` read set and write set per system for one system to every eight columns, a `Has` pick of every read, and a whole-registry row written into and read back from an `Arr<4>` product. The hooks library refuses a cell where a call built anything other than the fixture alone, or where cargo failed.

Not a design change: no crate's design says anything about benches. The harness's own files are new, under `mock/benches/`.
