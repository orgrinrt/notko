# src changelist

The bench gets a floor under the product build, and its check refuses a call at the wrong point or with the wrong fixture. The review of round 202611200639 named both.

## CHANGE: a floor arm, `cargo_alone`, under `from_clean`

`mock/benches/product-build/arms/cargo_alone/src/lib.rs` builds the fixture without its `registry` feature, so cargo starts, resolves, fingerprints and writes an empty library, and the compiler has nothing of the product to do. `bench.toml` names it as `floor`, so the harness subtracts it before every delta. `Fixture` in `product-build-call/src/call.rs` says which of the two a call builds.

## CHANGE: the check refuses a call at another point or with the wrong fixture

`record` writes the point and whether the registry was asked for and built, the last read from the features in cargo's own message. `verdict` in `record.rs` fails a cell where any call ran at another point than the cell's, or built the fixture with other features than it asked for. `tests/one_call.rs` builds both fixtures from clean and asserts the verdict passes at its point and fails at another, and fails over a call built without a clean; `record.rs` holds one test per spoiled field.

## CHANGE: the build directory is asked of cargo

`own_dir` takes the target directory from `cargo metadata` instead of assuming one, so the bench's directory follows the environment and the workspace config alike.

## CHANGE: the findings note states what its numbers are

`mock/research/202611200700_product-build-time/NOTES.md` says its medians are the harness's warm medians over a whole cargo invocation, quotes the floor and the compile time above it, and drops the extrapolation past the measured points.

## CHANGE: a correction to round 202611200639

Its locked src changelist says the init sample bench is removed. It was not; this round's topic records the correction, and the closed round is left as it was locked.
