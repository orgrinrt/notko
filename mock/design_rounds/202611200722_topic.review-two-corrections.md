## What the second review found

The second review of the branch (`mock/research/202611200700_product-build-time/REVIEW-2.md`) found that the bench says something about its floor that the harness does not do. `bench.toml` and the locked src changelist of round 202611200711 say the harness takes `floor` off `from_clean` before every delta. It does that only in its `ratio` normalise mode (mockspace `bench-harness/src/report.rs`, the `ratio_mode` branch); this bench runs in the default mode, so `report.md` shows raw deltas and the figures above the floor in NOTES.md were worked out by hand. The run stays as it is, its numbers are right; what is wrong is the sentence. The comment is corrected and NOTES.md says the subtraction is its own. The changelist of round 202611200711 is locked and is left as it is; this topic is its correction.

NOTES.md also says the earlier run's medians agree with this one within its ranges. They do not: each sits just below this run's lowest sample at its point, so the two runs differ by more than either run's own spread. The ratios and the exponent hold in both; the absolute figures carry that difference between runs as their uncertainty, and the note says so. Two figures were worked from rounded medians (the 128 row's 763 and +571, which are 762 and +570), and the floor's lowest sample is 42.7 ms, not 43.

The integration test's assertion that a verdict fails at another point repeats a unit test, since the point it passes is its own. It is replaced by a check over real output: the empty fixture built and recorded as if it had asked for the registry, which the verdict must refuse because cargo says it built without the feature.

Round 202611200711 was opened after the code it describes was committed and its run had started; the run names that commit, so the order is recorded here rather than rewritten. Two things the review named are left as tickets: the two arms are near copies whose shared body belongs in the support crate, and the integration test writes to the bench's own record and target directory, so it must not run during a bench run.
