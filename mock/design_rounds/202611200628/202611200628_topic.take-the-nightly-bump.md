# take-the-nightly-bump


The nightly bump (`3359390`, `chore: move notko to the 2026-11-18 nightly`) landed on `dev` while this branch moved the crates under `mock/crates/`. Merging `dev` here brings its `.stderr` updates, two new compile-fail files and three edited test sources onto the moved paths, and the root pin `nightly-2026-11-18`.

What this round changes: nothing of its own in source. The merge takes `dev`'s side of every touched test file at its new path, and `mock/rust-toolchain.toml` follows the root pin, since the lint `the-mock-toolchain-matches-the-root` refuses a mock toolchain that shadows the root's. The brief says the bump's pin wins on a conflict.
