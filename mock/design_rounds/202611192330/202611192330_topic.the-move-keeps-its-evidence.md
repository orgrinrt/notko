# The move keeps its evidence

Round 202611192252 moved the crates and the research under `mock/`. The commit that did the move, `9968e5a`, was staged as renames, and the commit hook formats every staged Rust file, so 141 of the moved files were rewritten on the way: the trybuild cases of `notko-hlist`, and the probes and sketches under `mock/research/`.

Two kinds of damage came of it. Eight compile-fail cases of `notko-hlist` moved their lines while the blessed `.stderr` beside them still quoted the old ones, so `the_refusals_hold` went red on the branch where it is green on `dev`. And committed evidence changed under its recorded output: a probe written to test the `~const` spelling now spells `[const]`, and several `.out.txt` files quote lines that are no longer there. A sketch is evidence of what was run, and a probe that changes after its run is no longer the thing that was run.

The round's own verification said every arm and `cargo mock test` were green. That held for the tree before the hook's rewrite and not for the tree it locked, so the claim was false at lock time.

The fix is to put every one of those files back to its bytes on `dev`, so the move is a move, and to keep the formatter out of the two places where bytes are the point. rustfmt reads the nearest `rustfmt.toml` up from each file, so a `rustfmt.toml` with `disable_all_formatting = true` in `mock/research/` and in each trybuild case directory stops the hook there without touching the shared config at the root. The hook formatting a pure rename is a mockspace defect and is ticketed there (`the_commit_hook_s_rustfmt_moves_a_sketch_s_trybuild_fixtures`).

No design changes. The doc changelist of this round says so and nothing else.
